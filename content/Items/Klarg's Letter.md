This is what exposed the plot to kidnap [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] (referred to here only as "the dwarf") and connected [[Session Notes/Quests/Goblin Infestation|Goblin Infestation]] to [[Session Notes/Quests/Finding Gundren|Finding Gundren]].

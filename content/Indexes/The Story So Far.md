## Session 0

Character introductions and setting discussion. The party formed and began traveling toward [[Phandalin Overview|Phandalin]].

## Session 1

The wagon to Phandalin was ambushed by four goblins ([[Session Notes/Session 1#Combat|Session 1]]); the party fought them off. In Phandalin, they delivered goods to [[Locations/Phandalin/Shop - Barthen's Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]] and visited [[Locations/Phandalin/Shop - Lionshield Coster|Lionshield Coster]], where [[Linene Graywind]] asked them to deal with goblins raiding shipments in exchange for 50 gp ([[Session Notes/Quests/Goblin Infestation|Goblin Infestation]]). At the [[Locations/Phandalin/Inn - Stonehill Inn|Stonehill Inn]], they picked up a lead from [[Pip]] about a hidden tunnel into the goblin camp, confirmed the next day by [[Carp]] at [[Locations/Phandalin/Residence - Alderleaf Farm|Alderleaf Farm]]. The party located the goblin hideout entrance and fought some goblins just outside ([[Session Notes/Session 1#Combat|Session 1]]), ending the session preparing to enter the cave.

## Session 2

The party tried to enter a cave. The inhabitants were not thrilled, and chose to attempt geronticide, much to the dismay of Mophlin and Alvar. Hilda was too busy being unconscious and almost dead, while Willow frankly wasn't all that bothered. After recollecting themselves, they re-entered the cave and began slowly taking out those inside with the help of some wolves that had been previously imprisoned near the entrance.

## Session 3

The party pushed deeper into the [[Locations/Phandalin/The Goblin Hideout|goblin hideout]], clearing the remaining rooms with help from Mophlin's freed [[Wolves (Freed Pack)|wolf pack]], losing one wolf in the fighting, and finishing off [[Bestiary/Klarg|Klarg]], the bugbear chief guarding the back rooms. Searching the lair turned up a new Morningstar for Hilda and [[Items/Klarg's Letter|a letter]] revealing Klarg's plan to kidnap [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]], one of Alvar's friends; following that lead, they found and freed [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]], a second kidnapping victim already being held in the cave, and agreed to escort him back to Phandalin for 50 gp. The party levelled up during the fighting.

## Session 4

[[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] opened up about why he's really in the area: he's searching for [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] and [[Iano Albreck|Iano Albreck]], a missing companion, and he's an agent of the [[Groups/Lords Alliance|Lords Alliance]]. He shared a troubling lead: a strange goblin had told him he "wasn't who they wanted," and mentioned [[Wave Echo Cave|Wave Echo Cave]], a place Gundren had apparently found before vanishing. When pressed, Sildar said someone calling themself "[[Session Notes/Rumors/The Spider|The Spider]]" had asked to see Gundren before he was taken away to what we assume was the manor in Cragmore based on this [[Klarg's Letter|letter]].

## Session 5

The party returned [[Linene Graywind|Linene]]'s goods and collected the 50 gp for clearing out the goblins. She warned them about the [[Groups/Redbrands|Redbrands]], mercenaries helping the goblins who are staying at the Sleeping Giant taphouse, and said they had been asking about old mines. At Barthen's Provisions, [[Elmina]] turned out to be a friend of Gundren, and only talked after [[Player Characters/Alvar/Alvar Mistwater|Alvar]] pleaded with her, telling the party that wherever the Redbrands are, they would likely find Gundren. At the Shrine of Tymora, [[NPCs/Sister Garaele]], who works for the [[Groups/Harpers|Harpers]], examined Willow's dagger and offered to investigate it, and to help Mophlin talk to his wolves, if the party persuades a [[Compendium/Monsters Library#Banshee|banshee]] named [[Bestiary/Agatha|Agatha]] to say where a spellbook is. The party then went after the Redbrands at the taphouse (Willow started the fight by throwing a toy at one), killed at least two, and knocked out the last. The taphouse's owner told them the Redbrands' hideout, where they take prisoners, is the old [[Locations/Phandalin/Tressinder Manor|Tressinder Manor]] on the hill, and the orc bartender offered to help when they go.

# The Story So Far

## Session 0

Character introductions and setting discussion. The party formed and began traveling toward [[Phandalin Overview|Phandalin]].

## Session 1

The wagon to Phandalin was ambushed by four goblins ([[Session 1 - Goblin Skirmish]]); the party fought them off. In Phandalin, they delivered goods to [[Locations/Phandalin/Shop - Barthen's Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]] and visited [[Locations/Phandalin/Shop - Lionshield Coster|Lionshield Coster]], where [[Linene Graywind]] asked them to deal with goblins raiding shipments in exchange for 50 gp ([[Session Notes/Quests/Goblin Infestation|Goblin Infestation]]). At the [[Locations/Phandalin/Inn - Stonehill Inn|Stonehill Inn]], they picked up a lead from [[Pip]] about a hidden tunnel into the goblin camp, confirmed the next day by [[Carp]] at [[Locations/Phandalin/Residence - Alderleaf Farm|Alderleaf Farm]]. The party located the goblin hideout entrance and fought some goblins just outside ([[Session 1 - Goblin Hideout Entrance]]), ending the session preparing to enter the cave.

## Session 2

The party tried to enter a cave. The inhabitants were not thrilled, and chose to attempt geronticide, much to the dismay of Mophlin and Alvar. Hilda was too busy being unconscious and almost dead, while Willow frankly wasn't all that bothered. After recollecting themselves, they re-entered the cave and began slowly taking out those inside with the help of some wolves that had been previously imprisoned near the entrance.

## Session 3

The party pushed deeper into the [[Locations/Phandalin/The Goblin Hideout|goblin hideout]], clearing the remaining rooms with help from Mophlin's freed [[Wolves (Freed Pack)|wolf pack]] — losing one wolf in the fighting — and finishing off the [[Monsters Library#Bugbears|bugbear]] chief guarding the back rooms. Searching the lair turned up a new morningstar for Hilda and a letter revealing the bugbear's plan to kidnap one of Alvar's friends; following that lead, they found and freed a second kidnapping victim already being held in the cave and agreed to escort him back to Phandalin for 50 gp. The party leveled up during the fighting.

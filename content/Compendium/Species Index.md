# Species Index

Every playable species in the compendium, linking to its full write-up. This is navigation, not new content; everything here already exists in `Compendium/Character Origins.md`.

The first 10 are the core 2024 Player's Handbook species. The rest are expansion species (Volo's Guide to Monsters, Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes, Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse, and Eberron: Rising from the Last War); each is tagged with its sourcebook in the compendium entry itself.

> [!tip] Giving an NPC a race
> Every species below also has a short quick-reference page under `Compendium/Races/` (size, speed, condensed traits, a link back to the full compendium entry, and a live table of who's that species). Link an NPC's race to its page there, e.g. `[[Compendium/Races/Human|Human]]` or `[[Compendium/Races/Tiefling|Tiefling]]`, instead of linking straight into the compendium, so the NPC shows up in that species' own "Known Party Members / NPCs" table.
>
> **No Half-Elf or Half-Orc:** the 2024 rules dropped both as separate species. Elf and Orc absorbed them as flavor rather than mechanics. An NPC described as "half-elf" can freely use the [[Compendium/Races/Elf|Elf]] or [[Compendium/Races/Human|Human]] page (DM's call which traits fit), noting the mixed heritage in prose.

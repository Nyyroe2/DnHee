#

A running list of in-world vocabulary as it comes up

**Dawnbringer**: Title given to senior clergy of the Church of Lathander who've mastered its divine gifts. See [[Deities#Lathander|Lathander]].

**The Triad**: Collective term for [[Deities#Tyr|Tyr]], [[Deities#Torm|Torm]], and [[Deities#Ilmater|Ilmater]], three lawful good deities whose faiths frequently cooperate.

**The Weave**: The underlying fabric that makes arcane spellcasting possible, embodied by [[Deities#Mystra|Mystra]].

# Deities

> [!info]- About this page
> Information is summarized based on the 2024 rules . See [[The Faerûnian Pantheon]] for a full browsable index including minor deities not detailed on this page.

## Lathander, The Morninglord

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Good |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]], [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Light Domain\|Light]] |
| Symbol | Rising sun, usually rendered in gold and red, with rose and violet as associated colors |
| Portfolio | Dawn, birth, renewal, self-perfection |

God of dawn, birth, renewal, and self-perfection. Clergy are typically called Dawnbringers once ordained. The mace (traditionally called the Dawnspeaker in older lore) is his favored weapon, which is why it's standard-issue for Lathanderite clergy.

### Faith and Beliefs

Dawn as a daily proof that ruin is never final. Devotees are encouraged toward action over contemplation; missionary work, charity, and plain kindness are treated as valid expressions of devotion, on equal footing with miracle-working. The faith holds a particular antipathy toward necromancy and undeath, seeing the perversion of death and decay as the direct inverse of everything dawn represents.

### Relationships

Generally on good terms with other benevolent faiths. Lathander's most significant documented rival is [[Deities#Shar|Shar]], goddess of darkness, loss, and secrets, the literal narrative opposite of dawn.

### See Also

[[Church of Lathander]]: the specific temple and clergy Hilda belongs to.

## Tymora, Lady Luck

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Good |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]] |
| Symbol | A face-up silver coin |
| Portfolio | Good fortune, luck, adventure, skill |

Goddess of good fortune and risk-taking. Favored by anyone who makes a living gambling on outcomes – adventurers, gamblers, sailors, merchants who took a chance on a shipment.

### Profile

Worship tends to be casual and personal rather than formalized – a coin flipped before a risky venture, a quick prayer before a bet. Formal temples exist but shrines and personal devotion are more common than grand cathedrals.

### Faith and Beliefs

The core belief: fortune favors the bold, not the cautious. Tymora's faithful are encouraged to take chances rather than play it safe.

### Relationships

Her twin sister **Beshaba**, goddess of misfortune, is her natural opposite – the two are often invoked together, half-jokingly, when someone's luck could go either way.

## Chauntea, The Earth Mother

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Good |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]] |
| Symbol | A sheaf of grain, or a blooming rose over grain |
| Portfolio | Agriculture, farming, the harvest, life itself |

Goddess of agriculture and the natural bounty of the land. One of the most widely worshipped deities in the Realms – nearly every farming community keeps some shrine to her, whether or not anyone would call themselves formally devout.

### Profile

Rural, humble worship. Small shrines at field edges, first-harvest offerings, blessings asked over planting rather than grand temple rites.

### Faith and Beliefs

Life and growth are sacred; the land is to be tended, not just taken from. Farmers who work with the land rather than exhaust it are living her teaching whether they know her name or not.

### Relationships

Generally allied with other life-affirming and nature faiths (Silvanus, Mielikki). Opposed by anything that blights or poisons the land.

## Shar, The Lady of Loss, Mistress of the Night

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]], Death |
| Symbol | A black disk encircled by a border |
| Portfolio | Darkness, loss, secrets, forgetfulness |

Goddess of darkness and everything dawn is not – loss, secrets, the erasure of memory and self. Lathander's clearest and most direct opposite number.

### Profile

Worship is secretive almost by definition – hidden shrines, whispered rites, congregations that don't advertise themselves. Public Sharrans are rare.

### Faith and Beliefs

The teaching runs toward nihilism: light and memory and attachment all fade eventually, so better to embrace the dark and let go before it's taken from you.

### Relationships

The rivalry between Shar and Lathander is one of the most storied in the pantheon, tied to an event Realms scholars call the Dawn Cataclysm.

## Tempus, The Lord of Battles, the Foehammer

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | True Neutral |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: War Domain\|War]] |
| Symbol | An upright flaming sword |
| Portfolio | War, battle, honorable combat |

God of war itself, indifferent to which side is righteous – what matters to him is that a fight is fought with honor and courage, not the cause behind it.

### Profile

Worshipped openly by soldiers, mercenaries, and warriors on every side of a conflict. Battlefield shrines, pre-battle rites, honors paid to fallen enemies as much as fallen allies.

### Faith and Beliefs

Courage and honorable conduct in battle matter more than victory. Cowardice and needless cruelty are the real sins, not violence itself.

### Relationships

Not aligned with good or evil factions specifically – soldiers of opposing armies might invoke him before the same battle.

## Helm, The Watcher

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Neutral |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]], [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Light Domain\|Light]] |
| Symbol | A staring eye on an upright left gauntlet |
| Portfolio | Protection, guardianship, vigilance, duty |

God of protection and unwavering vigilance – the divine patron of anyone whose job is to stand guard over something and not waver, no matter how long the watch.

### Profile

Disciplined and dutiful rather than glamorous. Clergy often serve as literal guards – of gates, vaults, borders, sacred sites – treating the post itself as an act of worship.

### Faith and Beliefs

Duty fulfilled without deviation, even at personal cost. A Helmite doesn't abandon a post because the situation changed; the post is the point.

### Relationships

Generally respected across the pantheon for reliability rather than allied with any particular faction – a Helmite guard would take the duty seriously regardless of whose treasury or gate they'd been assigned to.

## Oghma, The Binder of What is Known

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | True Neutral |
| Domain(s) | Knowledge |
| Symbol | A blank scroll |
| Portfolio | Knowledge, invention, bards, lore-keeping |

God of knowledge and the spread of it – patron of scholars, sages, bards, and anyone who values a well-kept record over a lost one.

### Profile

Worship centers on libraries, archives, and the act of teaching. Clergy often function as librarians and historians as much as priests.

### Faith and Beliefs

Knowledge should be gathered, preserved, and shared – hoarding it or letting it die with its keeper is treated as a genuine failing.

### Relationships

Loosely allied with any faith that values truth and learning. No strong rivalries – Oghma's faith tends to stay above factional politics.

## Selûne, The Moonmaiden

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Good |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]], [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Light Domain\|Light]] |
| Symbol | A pair of eyes surrounded by seven stars |
| Portfolio | The moon, stars, navigation, good-aligned lycanthropes |

Goddess of the moon and the light it casts in darkness – a guide for travelers and sailors navigating by night, and a protector against the corrupting dark.

### Profile

Night-time rites tied to the moon's phases. Temples often built with open roofs or towers for direct moonlight.

### Faith and Beliefs

Light can exist within darkness rather than only opposing it – a subtly different take on light-and-dark themes than Lathander's dawn-focused faith.

### Relationships

Her sister **Shar** is her opposite and rival – the two are often described as having been locked in conflict since the world's creation.

## Mystra, The Lady of Mysteries

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Good |
| Domain(s) | Knowledge |
| Symbol | A circle of seven stars around a flowing red mist, or simply a seven-pointed star |
| Portfolio | Magic itself, the Weave |

Goddess of magic – not just a patron of magic-users but the literal embodiment of the Weave, the underlying fabric that makes arcane spellcasting possible at all.

### Profile

Worshipped by wizards and sages of virtually any alignment; magic users often pay her respect regardless of their personal faith, given how directly she's tied to their power actually functioning.

### Faith and Beliefs

Magic is a gift to be used responsibly – Mystra has historically taken direct, personal interest in preventing catastrophic misuse of magic on a world-altering scale.

### Relationships

Works closely with Oghma; broadly opposed by anyone seeking to seize or corrupt the Weave itself rather than simply use it.

| Alignment | Lawful Good |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: War Domain\|War]], Knowledge |
| Symbol | A pair of balanced scales resting on a warhammer |

God of justice, blind and missing a hand – the maiming is central to his myth, a permanent reminder of the cost of upholding fairness even when it isn't easy or safe.

### Profile

Worshipped by judges, magistrates, and paladins. Clergy often function in a judicial capacity, settling disputes.

### Faith and Beliefs

Justice applied evenly, without favoritism, even when it's costly to the one enforcing it.

### Relationships

Forms a trio with **Torm** and **Ilmater**, sometimes called the Triad – three lawful good gods whose faiths frequently cooperate.

## Bane, The Black Hand, the Lord of Darkness

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: War Domain\|War]] |
| Symbol | An upright black right hand, clawed and clenched |
| Portfolio | Tyranny, hatred, fear, control |

God of tyranny – rule through fear and absolute control rather than any pretense of fairness. One of the most commonly invoked patrons of genuinely villainous organizations across the Realms.

### Profile

Worship tends to appear wherever petty tyrants and oppressive regimes need a doctrine to justify themselves – cults of personality, strict hierarchies enforced through terror.

### Faith and Beliefs

Power justifies its own use. The strong rule the weak, and that's simply the correct order of things.

### Relationships

Rival to virtually every good-aligned deity by nature; frequently allied in fiction and lore with other tyrannical or destructive gods.

### Notes

Worth recognizing on sight – Bane's symbol showing up anywhere is a strong signal of genuine, organized villainy rather than simple banditry.

## Ilmater, The Crying God

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Good |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]] |
| Symbol | Two hands bound at the wrist by a red cord |
| Portfolio | Endurance, suffering, martyrdom, perseverance |

God of endurance through suffering – patron of the oppressed, the enslaved, and anyone enduring hardship not by choice but by circumstance.

### Profile

Clergy often work directly with the suffering: healers, prison ministers, those who tend the poor and the tortured. Some orders practice voluntary suffering in solidarity.

### Faith and Beliefs

Suffering endured without breaking, and relieved wherever possible in others, is itself a holy act.

### Relationships

Part of the Triad with **Tyr** and **Torm** – where they emphasize justice and courage, Ilmater's faith emphasizes compassion for those justice hasn't yet reached.

## Silvanus, Oak Father, the Forest Father

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | True Neutral |
| Domain(s) | Nature |
| Symbol | An oak leaf |
| Portfolio | Wild nature, untamed wilderness |

God of wild nature in its untamed form – less about cultivation like Chauntea, more about the forest that exists whether or not anyone tends it.

### Profile

Druidic circles and rangers are his most common devotees. Worship happens in the wild itself rather than in built temples.

### Faith and Beliefs

Nature's balance matters more than any individual outcome within it – a forest fire and a forest's growth are both part of the same cycle.

### Relationships

Close kinship with **Mielikki** and other nature-aligned deities; a natural counterpoint to anything that would pave over or exploit wilderness without limit.

Goddess of trade in the broadest sense – fair deals, smuggling, and everything in between, so long as goods and coin are actually changing hands.

### Profile

Popular with merchants, traders, and anyone whose livelihood depends on a good bargain. Worship often overlaps with simple good business sense.

### Faith and Beliefs

Wealth earned through exchange and negotiation is virtuous; wealth hoarded and taken out of circulation is against her nature.

### Relationships

Neutral toward most factions – a Waukeenar cares more about the deal than who's on the other side of it.

## Cyric, The Prince of Lies

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]] |
| Symbol | A white jawless skull on a black or purple sunburst |
| Portfolio | Murder, lies, intrigue, illusion |

God of murder, lies, and strife, one of the youngest deities in the pantheon, having clawed his way to godhood during the Time of Troubles by killing three older gods outright (Bhaal, Bane, and Myrkul). Widely regarded as the most dangerous and least stable deity in the Realms.

### Profile

Cyric's church is a font of paranoia by design: he demands his clergy compete viciously for his favor, and rewards betrayal of rivals within his own faith as readily as betrayal of outsiders. Assassins, liars, and the ambitious gravitate toward him.

### Faith and Beliefs

Truth is a weapon to be wielded, not a virtue to be upheld. Followers are taught that any lie which advances Cyric's cause, or their own standing within his church, is righteous by definition.

### Relationships

Despises nearly everyone, but reserves special hatred for **Mystra** (whose portfolio he covets) and **Kelemvor** (who inherited Myrkul's death portfolio instead of him). Uneasy, transactional alliances with **Mask** and **Shar** are common, and just as commonly betrayed.

### Notes

## Bahamut, The Platinum Dragon

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Good |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: War Domain\|War]] |
| Symbol | A dragon's head in profile, or a platinum dragon |
| Portfolio | Good dragons, justice, protection, nobility |

Father of good dragons and a patron of justice and nobility more broadly, worshipped by good-aligned dragonborn and metallic dragons across the multiverse, not exclusively tied to Faerûn the way most of this pantheon is.

### Profile

Bahamut is said to wander the Material Plane in mortal disguise (often as an old man with a platinum beard) to test the hearts of those he meets. His faith has few formal temples in Faerûn proper, existing more as a personal code among dragonborn and paladins than an organized religion.

### Faith and Beliefs

Strength exists to protect the weak, not dominate them. Justice should be tempered with mercy, but never abandoned for convenience.

### Relationships

Eternal enemy of **Tiamat**, his five-headed opposite; their rivalry predates the current cosmology and is treated as a near-metaphysical constant.

### Notes

Relevant to this table given Dragonborn is a playable species: a Bahamut-worshipping dragonborn PC or NPC would be an unremarkable, even expected, choice.

## Tiamat, The Nine-Hells Queen of Evil Dragons

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]], War |
| Symbol | A dragon's claw clutching five gems, one of each chromatic color |
| Portfolio | Evil dragons, greed, envy, wealth, vengeance |

Mother of evil dragons and a genuine cosmic power in her own right, currently imprisoned in the Nine Hells as part of a long-standing debt to Asmodeus, which does nothing to diminish how seriously her cults are taken.

### Profile

Her cults are typically secretive and cell-based rather than public temples, often built around hoarding wealth and cultivating personal grudges as much as outright worship. Chromatic dragons and kobolds venerate her more openly than most humanoids do.

### Faith and Beliefs

Greed is not a vice but a virtue; what you can take and hold is rightfully yours. Envy of what others have is simply correct appraisal.

### Relationships

Eternal enemy of **Bahamut**. Maintains a wary, transactional relationship with **Asmodeus** stemming from her imprisonment-debt in the Nine Hells.

## Talos, The Storm Lord, the Destroyer

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Evil |
| Domain(s) | Tempest |
| Symbol | Three lightning bolts radiating from a central point |
| Portfolio | Storms, destruction, natural disasters, rebellion against order |

God of storms and destruction, worshipped less out of devotion than out of fear. Coastal and mountain communities exposed to his wrath often maintain small shrines purely to be left alone.

### Profile

Talos has almost no organized clergy in the conventional sense; worship tends to be either fearful placation by ordinary folk, or genuine fanatical devotion by those who find purpose in tearing things down.

### Faith and Beliefs

Destruction is generative, not merely negative; the storm that levels the old village clears ground for something new, whether or not that's cold comfort to the villagers.

### Relationships

Loosely allied with the other members of the so-called Dark Three (**Bane**, **Myrkul** historically), though Talos's alliances are as unstable as everything else about him.

## Auril, The Frostmaiden

Goddess of winter's cruelty: not the changing of the seasons in the abstract, but the specific, personal malice of a blizzard that catches travelers exposed. Especially relevant to any Icewind Dale-adjacent or far-northern content.

Her faithful are rare and mostly found in the frozen North; the Ten Towns of Icewind Dale in particular have an uneasy, propitiatory relationship with her rather than one of genuine devotion.

## Kelemvor, The Lord of the Dead

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Neutral |
| Domain(s) | Death, [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Grave Domain\|Grave]] |
| Symbol | A vertical skeletal arm holding a set of balance scales |
| Portfolio | Death, the dead, judgment of souls |

God of death and judge of the dead, ruler of the Fugue Plane, where mortal souls go to be sorted according to their faith (or lack of it) in life. Rose to godhood during the Time of Troubles, taking the death portfolio from the murdered Myrkul.

### Profile

Kelemvor's faith is one of the more universally tolerated in the Realms: nearly everyone dies, and few begrudge the god whose entire function is making sure that process is orderly and fair rather than left to chance or corruption.

### Faith and Beliefs

Death is not evil, only natural and inevitable; what matters is whether it comes justly and whether the dead are shown proper respect afterward. Undeath is an abomination, a mockery of the order he was raised up specifically to enforce.

### Relationships

Bitter enemy of **Cyric**, who wanted the death portfolio for himself and has never stopped resenting losing it. Opposed on principle to any necromancer or lich, regardless of that individual's other allegiances.

## Gond, The Wonderbringer

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Forge Domain\|Forge]], Knowledge |
| Symbol | A toothed cog with four spokes |
| Portfolio | Craft, invention, smithwork, engineering |

God of craft and invention, patron of smiths, engineers, and tinkerers, with a reputation for favoring bold (occasionally reckless) experimentation over cautious, proven technique.

### Profile

The Church of Gond runs more like a guild than a conventional temple: workshops attached to shrines, apprenticeships treated as a form of religious service, and "Gondsmen" who travel selling inventions as much as preaching.

### Faith and Beliefs

Progress is itself a form of worship; a clever new device honors Gond more than a hymn does. Failure in pursuit of invention is an acceptable, even expected, cost.

### Relationships

Friendly rivalry with **Oghma** over whether raw knowledge or its practical application matters more. Generally well-regarded across the pantheon as harmless and useful.

## Mask, The Lord of Shadows

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]] |
| Symbol | A black mask |
| Portfolio | Thieves, thievery, shadows, intrigue |

Patron god of thieves and those who operate in shadow, less interested in grand cosmic evil than in the practical business of taking what isn't freely given.

### Profile

Worship is almost entirely clandestine: hidden shrines in thieves' guild halls, private rites rather than public temples. Most cities' criminal underworlds have at least a token nod to Mask somewhere in their operation.

### Faith and Beliefs

Anything not nailed down (and plenty that is) is fair game; skill in taking it is the only virtue that matters. Getting caught is the only real sin.

### Relationships

Old and complicated rivalry with **Cyric**, who briefly absorbed Mask's portfolio during the Time of Troubles before Mask reclaimed it. Occasional uneasy cooperation with **Vhaeraun**, whose portfolio overlaps significantly.

## Vhaeraun, The Masked Lord

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]] |
| Symbol | A black mask with two crossed swords |
| Portfolio | Drow males, thievery, treachery, surface raiding |

God of drow males specifically, a direct reaction against **Lolth**'s matriarchal Underdark order, championing drow who'd rather raid and reclaim the surface than serve the Spider Queen's priestesses.

### Profile

His worship is suppressed wherever Lolth's church holds power (which is most Underdark drow cities), making his faith inherently rebellious and secretive by necessity rather than preference.

### Faith and Beliefs

Drow deserve the surface world stolen from them in ages past, and the matriarchal order Lolth enforces is itself the true betrayal of drow potential, not an eternal truth.

### Relationships

Direct religious and political enemy of **Lolth** and her priestesses. Wary, occasional common cause with **Eilistraee**'s followers against Lolth's church, despite otherwise having nothing in common.

### Notes

Relevant given several playable species in this compendium (Duergar, Deep Gnome) have Underdark ties, even though none are drow specifically.

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Symbol | A sword-wielding, dancing drow female silhouetted against a full moon |

Goddess of good-aligned drow, offering a path of redemption for drow who reject Lolth's cruelty, encouraging them to reclaim the surface world through music, dance, and swordplay rather than raiding or conquest.

### Profile

Her worship exists almost entirely in secret, scattered surface shrines and hidden Underdark groves, actively hunted by Lolth's church wherever discovered, making a drow who follows her a marked individual by definition.

### Faith and Beliefs

Redemption is always possible, even for a people as thoroughly associated with evil as the drow; joy, art, and swordplay in moonlight are themselves sacred acts, not distractions from devotion.

### Relationships

Direct opposite of **Lolth**, and the deity her church most actively works to undermine. Sympathetic but distant relationship with **Vhaeraun**'s followers: shared enemy, incompatible methods.

## Lolth, The Spider Queen, the Demon Queen of Spiders

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]], War |
| Symbol | A black spider, sometimes with a female drow head |
| Portfolio | Drow, spiders, chaos, evil, assassins |

The dominant goddess of the drow, a demon lord elevated to full godhood, whose matriarchal priesthood effectively rules most Underdark drow society. Treats her own faithful as expendable pieces in a game only she fully understands.

### Profile

Her church is the drow ruling class in most Underdark cities: matron mothers and high priestesses hold both religious and civil authority simultaneously, and advancement within the church is explicitly encouraged to happen through betrayal and assassination of rivals.

### Faith and Beliefs

Chaos and betrayal are not flaws in the order of things but the order of things; loyalty is a tool for the weak to extract from others, never one to extend upward.

### Relationships

Enforces harsh suppression of both **Vhaeraun** and **Eilistraee** worship as existential threats to her control over drow society. Relationship with other demon lords and the Abyss generally is one of the few areas of genuine (if still treacherous) cooperation.

### Notes

Relevant given this compendium's Duergar and Deep Gnome species both have hostile Underdark history with drow expansion, even without a drow PC or NPC in play yet.

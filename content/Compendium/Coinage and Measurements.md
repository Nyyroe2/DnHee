# Coinage and Measurements

## Coinage

| Coin           | Abbreviation | Value            |
| -------------- | ------------ | ---------------- |
| Copper piece   | cp           | 1 cp (base unit) |
| Silver piece   | sp           | 10 cp            |
| Electrum piece | ep           | 5 sp (50 cp)     |
| Gold piece     | gp           | 10 sp (2 ep)     |
| Platinum piece | pp           | 10 gp            |

## Quick Conversions

- 1 gp = 10 sp = 100 cp
- 1 pp = 10 gp = 100 sp
- A skilled tradesperson might earn roughly 2 gp a day; a laborer closer to 2 sp a day

# Feat Index

Available feats, grouped by category, linking to full text in the compendium. Origin Feats are available at level 1 (some backgrounds grant one automatically); General Feats need level 4+; Fighting Style Feats are only for classes with a Fighting Style feature; Epic Boon Feats are level 19+.

This index covers both the core 2024 Player's Handbook feats and the expansion feats from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, Xanathar's Guide to Everything, Eberron: Rising from the Last War, and Fizban's Treasury of Dragons. Each expansion feat's compendium entry is tagged with its sourcebook; several of the Xanathar's-era feats also carry a species prerequisite, a legacy design the 2024 PHB dropped.

# Agatha

### "The Banshee"

> _A [[Compendium/Monsters Library#Banshee|banshee]] Garaele wants questioned about a mage's spellbook._

**Undead** · From: Not yet discovered · Status: Undead · Trust: Suspicious

## What We've Learned

[[NPCs/Sister Garaele]] says Agatha is a banshee who knows where a spellbook is, one that used to belong to a powerful mage. Garaele thinks flattery and compliments are the way to get information out of her, and that she'll trade an answer for a gift, one question per gift. Agatha attacked Garaele because Garaele reminded her of her old love.

## Known For

- Being the target of the [[Session Notes/Quests/Agatha's Spellbook|Agatha's Spellbook]] quest. The party has not met her yet ([[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]]).
- Attacking [[NPCs/Sister Garaele]] over a resemblance to her old love.

## Relationships

**[[NPCs/Sister Garaele]]**: Attacked her for resembling Agatha's old love.

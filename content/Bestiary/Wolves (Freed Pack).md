# Wolves (Freed Pack)

**Beast** · **Manageable** · From: [[Locations/Phandalin/The Goblin Hideout|The Goblin Hideout]] · Status: Alive · Trust: Trusted

## First Encounter

Three wolves found chained near the entrance to [[Locations/Phandalin/The Goblin Hideout|The Goblin Hideout]], abused by one of the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]] captors, and a fourth that was kept deeper inside the hideout. [[Player Characters/Mophlin/Mophlin Grimsbourne|Mophlin]] freed them and destroyed the whip used on them; they're now allied with the party rather than hostile. One of the four died in the fight against the goblins within, which leaves three.

## Known For

- [[Session Notes/Session 2|Session 2]]: The wolves were found and freed, then Mophlin assisted in killing their former captor; one bit Willow after being alerted, but stood down once it recognized the rest of the pack was safe.
- [[Session Notes/Session 3|Session 3]]: One of the wolves was killed in the fight against the goblins. Mophlin covered him with his cloak and he and Hilda held a funeral for him.
- [[Session Notes/Session 4|Session 4]]: The wolves now follow Mophlin around, much to the dismay of local bartenders everywhere.
- [[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]]: Mophlin has three wolves with him, not two as previously recorded. There were four to begin with, counting one that was kept in the hideout and missed in earlier notes. They dragged away a Redbrand he had pinned to a table with his greataxe and ate him.

## Relationships

**[[Player Characters/Mophlin/Mophlin Grimsbourne|Mophlin]]**: Freed them from captivity; they now follow him as pack.

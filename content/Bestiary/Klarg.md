# Klarg

![[miscImgs/Klarg.png|634]]

### "The Bugbear Chief"

> _Ran the goblin hideout's back rooms and the kidnapping plot, and died to Hilda's sacred flame in Session 3._

**Humanoid** · **Dangerous** · From: [[Locations/Phandalin/The Goblin Hideout|The Goblin Hideout]] · Status: Dead · Trust: Hostile

## First Encounter

Found commanding the storeroom deep in [[Locations/Phandalin/The Goblin Hideout|the goblin hideout]] (Session 3), fighting alongside goblins and a personal wolf companion.

## What We've Learned

The bugbear chief running the goblin hideout's back rooms, who fought with a wolf companion. He was behind the kidnapping plot against [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]; [[Items/Klarg's Letter|the letter]] the party found in his lair is what exposed it.

## Known For

- Orchestrating the plot to kidnap [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] on behalf of parties still unidentified ([[Items/Klarg's Letter|the letter]] mentions "Cragmaw Castle" and "the black spider"; see [[Session Notes/Rumors/The Spider|The Spider]]).
- Killed by Hilda's sacred flame while defending Mophlin ([[Session Notes/Session 3|Session 3]]). His wolf survived and was talked down rather than killed.

# Lords Alliance

## Overview

**Type**: Government / Political Alliance

**Alignment**: Lawful Neutral

**Party Standing**: Friendly

![Placeholder Iconography](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/36b98e212e9d73fe1bd4813f96b0fd71/z_Assets/Misc/ImagePlaceholder.png)

A loose alliance between a number of the North's major cities and towns, such as Waterdeep and Neverwinter, that cooperates on keeping the roads safe and trade moving. It doesn't rule anyone directly; each member city still runs its own affairs. Most people have heard the name without ever having dealt with it personally. [[Sildar Hallwinter]] is the first agent of theirs the party has actually met.

## Profile

Respected more than loved. People assume it's doing something useful, but few know what. Most folk only see its effects, like a well-patrolled trade road or a bounty notice.

## Powers and Duties

Officially: coordinating defense and trade between member settlements, and dealing with threats too big for any one town to handle alone. Unofficially, a citizen's guess is as good as anyone's; the Alliance is known to use agents who don't always announce themselves as such, which is part of why Sildar hadn't mentioned it until he felt safe enough to.

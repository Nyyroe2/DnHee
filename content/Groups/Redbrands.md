# Redbrands

## Overview

**Type**: Mercenaries

**Employer**: Not yet discovered

**Party Standing**: Hostile

Mercenaries who have been helping the goblins around [[Phandalin Overview|Phandalin]]. [[Linene Graywind|Linene]] told the party they stay at the [[Locations/Phandalin/Taphouse - Sleeping Giant|Sleeping Giant taphouse]], and that they would definitely be missed if something happened to them.

## Profile

The party first heard of them from Linene in [[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]]. They had been into her shop asking for information about old mines, specifically the old Phandelver mines (see [[Wave Echo Cave]]). [[Elmina]] thinks that wherever they are, the party is likely to find [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]].

## Story

They had taken over the taproom of the Sleeping Giant, and the party fought several of them there in Session 5. Three were killed and the last was knocked out. With the place cleared, the party rested in the taproom. The taphouse's owner then told the party their hideout is the old [[Locations/Phandalin/Tressinder Manor|Tressinder Manor]] on the hill, where they take prisoners.

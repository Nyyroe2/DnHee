# Mophlin Grimsbourne

### The Wordless Berserker

> _"Small enough to underestimate. Enemies rarely get the chance to reconsider"_

**[[Compendium/Races/Gnome|Gnome]]** · **[[Classes#Barbarian|Barbarian]] / [[Classes#Paladin|Paladin]]** · **Alignment**: Chaotic Good · **Faith**: Unknown

**Status**: Alive

## Appearance

Not yet discovered.

## Personality & Demeanor

An endearing gnome who is only able to babble incoherently. He seems friendly if not a bit chaotic.

## Background

Unknown - Though he seems reluctant to share his name easily.

## NPC Relationships

**[[Wolves (Freed Pack)|The freed wolves]]**: Freed three wolves chained near the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]] captor's abuse in the cave, and destroyed the whip used on them. They fought alongside the party afterward until one of them was killed off in the final goblin fight in [[Session Notes/Session 3|Session 3]].  He has three wolves with him now, and they continue to fight by his side.

## Trivia

- Though he is able to read and write, he's unable to speak any tangible language and speaks only in gibberish
- He is a clear animal lover
- He uses pages from his journal to ask for help, like "can you help me speak to my dogs please" (Session 5)

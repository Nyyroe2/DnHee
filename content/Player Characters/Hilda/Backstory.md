Hilda has belonged to the Church of Lathander since infancy, left as a baby at the temple's orphanage and raised on prayer, scripture, and the daily miracle of sunrise. She took her vows the moment she was old enough and threw herself into service with devotion, but while her fellow acolytes went on to preform magic feats and miracles with Lathander's light, Hilda's divine gifts never grew past a spark. A little light, a little healing, nothing more, no matter how hard she prayed or how faithfully she served.

She spent her younger centuries on missionary trips and pilgrimages across the continent, certain that somewhere out in the world she'd finally find whatever made a true Dawnbringer. When that didn't work, she came home and tried _everything_ else, charity work,  volunteering at temple kitchens, teaching scripture to orphans who reminded her of herself, always quietly hoping devotion through service would count for as much as devotion through miracles. Mostly, she still believes that. But 349 years is a long time to almost-succeed at the one thing you built your life around.

Now, with her 350th birthday closing in, Hilda has packed up her mace and shield for one last pilgrimage. Publicly, she'll tell you it's because the world is drifting into darkness and someone devoted to Lathander ought to do something about all this shameless necromancy before it's too late. She does mean it, but she also recognizes that it's secretly one final, stubborn attempt to be useful to her god the way she's always believed she could be, before she has to accept that a candle's worth of light was always going to be the whole of it.

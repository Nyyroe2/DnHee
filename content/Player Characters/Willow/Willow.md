# Willow

### Daughter of the Woods

> _"Carries the forest's kindness in her, and a little of its wildness too."_

**[[Compendium/Races/Elf (Wood Elf)|Wood Elf]]** · **[[Classes#Druid|Druid]]** · **Alignment**: Unknown · **Faith**: Unknown

**Status**: Alive

## Appearance

A young adult wood elf with light, freckled skin, dark blonde hair, and green eyes.

## Personality & Demeanor

She seems quite aloof and unaware of how the world works, likely from being raised in the woods, but she seems to mean well and has a special care for animals and nature. Underneath the aloofness she is curious and friendly, and more sociable than she first appears. Willow is calm, kind and somewhat reserved, and she enjoys meeting new people.

## Background

Willow was found as a baby under an ancient tree deep in the forest, wrapped in a torn piece of cloth with a small golden dagger beside her. Nobody knows who left her there, and the dagger has been with her ever since.

The forest's animals raised her. Wolves taught her how to survive, birds taught her to listen, and the cats became her closest companions. She knows the forest better than any person could, and it is her home, her family and, more and more, her responsibility.

She isn't a stranger to people. She has picked up Common and visited the nearby towns often enough to know how things work outside the trees: finding an inn, buying supplies, reading signs, holding a decent conversation. She doesn't understand every custom, but she will ask when she's unsure. She likes hearing stories, learning new things, and sitting in a busy tavern to watch people go about their day.

Her days are for reading, crafting, gathering herbs and looking after animals. At night she patrols with her feline companions and watches for anyone who threatens the forest.

The dagger is her biggest mystery. She has no idea where it came from or why it chose her, and lately it glows more often. Animals near the oldest part of the forest have started to disappear, and Willow has begun to wonder whether the forest raised her for a reason and not simply because it was home. She means to find out, and to protect the only family she's ever had while she does.

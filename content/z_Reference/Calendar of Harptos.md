# The Calendar of Harptos

The standard calendar used across most of Faerûn — useful for dating session notes, journal entries, and figuring out how much in-world time has actually passed between sessions.

## Structure

365 days a year: twelve 30-day months, plus five extra "special days" that fall between certain months and belong to no month at all.

## Notes

The only religious place we've discovered in Phandalin has been a small shrine to Tymora that [[Linene Graywind]] mentioned she occasionally prays to.

The party met [[NPCs/Sister Garaele]] here in [[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]], a half-elf priestess who works for the [[Groups/Harpers|Harpers]]. She examined Willow's dagger and offered to investigate it in exchange for a favor (see [[Session Notes/Quests/Agatha's Spellbook|Agatha's Spellbook]]). [[Linene Graywind|Linene]] had recommended Mophlin visit the shrine.

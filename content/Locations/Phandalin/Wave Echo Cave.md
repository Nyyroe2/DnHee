As [[Sildar Hallwinter]] told them in Session 4, [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] found it, but whatever he learned was lost when he went missing. Sildar mentioned it holds something called the "Forge of Spells," tied to an old agreement he called the **Phandelver's Pact**, which he said let people of all races use the forge. He seemed to think that's why Gundren was taken.

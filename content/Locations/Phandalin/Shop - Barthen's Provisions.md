## First Impressions

A practical general store, nothing fancy. The shopkeeper comes across as kind.

## Description

A small basic goods shop in Phandalin, run by [[Elmina]]. The wagon [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] hired Alvar to escort was bound here, with ten gold pieces promised for its safe delivery.

## Notes

The party made a delivery here on arrival to Phandalin. The shopkeeper seems kind, and gave Mophlin a book and quill to help him communicate.

In [[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]] Alvar came back to check what had been in the wagon and press for more information. Elmina turned out to be a friend of Gundren, and she shut down when the party questioned her. After Alvar pleaded with her and answered her personal questions, she said that wherever the [[Groups/Redbrands|Redbrands]] are, the party would likely find Gundren.

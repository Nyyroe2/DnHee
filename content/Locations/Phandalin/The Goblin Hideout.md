## Overview

A cave system serving as a [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]] lair, entered via a hidden tunnel Pip and Carp both pointed the party toward. A stream runs through part of it; the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] have rigged it to swell and flood the entrance on demand.

## First Impressions

Damp, cramped, and hostile from the moment you step in — a stream runs through part of the cave, and the goblins have learned to weaponize it, flooding the entrance on demand to keep intruders out.

## Notable Areas

- **Entrance cave** - where the party first fought their way in ([[Session 1 - Goblin Hideout Entrance]]).
- **Wolf pen** - three wolves chained near the entrance, abused by their [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]] captor before being freed by [[Player Characters/Mophlin/Mophlin Grimsbourne|Mophlin]]. See [[Wolves (Freed Pack)]].
- **Trash chute** - a vertical passage up from the wolf pen, used to bypass the flooded stream.
- **Storeroom** - reached via the chute; a fire pit and at least one [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]] stationed inside.

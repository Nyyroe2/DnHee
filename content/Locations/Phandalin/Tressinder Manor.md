The old Tressinder Manor, on the hill above [[Phandalin Overview|Phandalin]]. The owner of the [[Locations/Phandalin/Taphouse - Sleeping Giant|Sleeping Giant taphouse]] told the party in [[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]] that it's the [[Groups/Redbrands|Redbrands]]' hideout, and where they take prisoners. [[Elmina]] said that wherever the Redbrands are, the party is likely to find [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]].

## Notes

Not yet visited. The orc bartender at the taphouse has offered to help the party when they go.

"The manor in Cragmore" was only a descriptor the party had been using. This is the manor [[Carp]] stumbled onto in Session 1, the one [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] described in Session 4 as where [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] was taken, and the one [[Items/Klarg's Letter|Klarg's letter]] points toward. Its real name is Tressinder Manor.

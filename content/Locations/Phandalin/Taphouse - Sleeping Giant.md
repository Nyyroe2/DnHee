## First Impressions

The owner and the bartender both seemed glad to see the [[Groups/Redbrands|Redbrands]] dealt with.

## Description

A taphouse in Phandalin whose taproom the Redbrands had taken over. The owner is a woman who hasn't given her name yet, and the bartender is a large orc.

## Notes

The party fought the Redbrands here in [[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]] and broke the doors doing it. The bartender called for the fight to be taken outside, and afterward the owner thanked the party and told them the Redbrands' hideout is [[Locations/Phandalin/Tressinder Manor|Tressinder Manor]]. Alvar helped repair the doors and turned down the 5 gp they offered him, so the orc bartender offered to help the party at the manor. With the mercenaries cleared out, the party rested in the taproom.

## Lathander, The Morninglord

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Good |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]], [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Light Domain\|Light]] |
| Symbol | Rising sun, usually rendered in gold and red, with rose and violet as associated colors |
| Portfolio | Dawn, birth, renewal, self-perfection |

God of dawn, birth, renewal, and self-perfection. Clergy are typically called Dawnbringers once ordained. The mace — traditionally called the Dawnspeaker in older lore — is his favored weapon, which is why it's standard-issue for Lathanderite clergy.

### Faith and Beliefs

Dawn as a daily proof that ruin is never final. Devotees are encouraged toward action over contemplation — missionary work, charity, and plain kindness are treated as valid expressions of devotion, on equal footing with miracle-working. The faith holds a particular antipathy toward necromancy and undeath, seeing the perversion of death and decay as the direct inverse of everything dawn represents.

### Relationships

Generally on good terms with other benevolent faiths. Lathander's most significant documented rival is [[z_5.5e Compendium/Deities#Shar|Shar]], goddess of darkness, loss, and secrets — the literal narrative opposite of dawn.

## Tymora, Lady Luck

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Good |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]] |
| Symbol | A face-up silver coin |
| Portfolio | Good fortune, luck, adventure, skill |

Goddess of good fortune and risk-taking. Favored by anyone who makes a living gambling on outcomes – adventurers, gamblers, sailors, merchants who took a chance on a shipment.

### Profile

Worship tends to be casual and personal rather than formalized – a coin flipped before a risky venture, a quick prayer before a bet. Formal temples exist but shrines and personal devotion are more common than grand cathedrals.

### Faith and Beliefs

The core belief: fortune favors the bold, not the cautious. Tymora's faithful are encouraged to take chances rather than play it safe.

### Relationships

Her twin sister **Beshaba**, goddess of misfortune, is her natural opposite – the two are often invoked together, half-jokingly, when someone's luck could go either way.

## Chauntea, The Earth Mother

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Good |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]] |
| Symbol | A sheaf of grain, or a blooming rose over grain |
| Portfolio | Agriculture, farming, the harvest, life itself |

Goddess of agriculture and the natural bounty of the land. One of the most widely worshipped deities in the Realms – nearly every farming community keeps some shrine to her, whether or not anyone would call themselves formally devout.

### Profile

Rural, humble worship. Small shrines at field edges, first-harvest offerings, blessings asked over planting rather than grand temple rites.

### Faith and Beliefs

Life and growth are sacred; the land is to be tended, not just taken from. Farmers who work with the land rather than exhaust it are living her teaching whether they know her name or not.

### Relationships

Generally allied with other life-affirming and nature faiths (Silvanus, Mielikki). Opposed by anything that blights or poisons the land.

## Shar, The Lady of Loss, Mistress of the Night

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]], Death |
| Symbol | A black disk encircled by a border |
| Portfolio | Darkness, loss, secrets, forgetfulness |

Goddess of darkness and everything dawn is not – loss, secrets, the erasure of memory and self. Lathander's clearest and most direct opposite number.

### Profile

Worship is secretive almost by definition – hidden shrines, whispered rites, congregations that don't advertise themselves. Public Sharrans are rare.

### Faith and Beliefs

The teaching runs toward nihilism: light and memory and attachment all fade eventually, so better to embrace the dark and let go before it's taken from you.

### Relationships

The rivalry between Shar and Lathander is one of the most storied in the pantheon, tied to an event Realms scholars call the Dawn Cataclysm.

## Tempus, The Lord of Battles, the Foehammer

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | True Neutral |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: War Domain\|War]] |
| Symbol | An upright flaming sword |
| Portfolio | War, battle, honorable combat |

God of war itself, indifferent to which side is righteous – what matters to him is that a fight is fought with honor and courage, not the cause behind it.

### Profile

Worshipped openly by soldiers, mercenaries, and warriors on every side of a conflict. Battlefield shrines, pre-battle rites, honors paid to fallen enemies as much as fallen allies.

### Faith and Beliefs

Courage and honorable conduct in battle matter more than victory. Cowardice and needless cruelty are the real sins, not violence itself.

### Relationships

Not aligned with good or evil factions specifically – soldiers of opposing armies might invoke him before the same battle.

## Helm, The Watcher

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Neutral |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]], [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Light Domain\|Light]] |
| Symbol | A staring eye on an upright left gauntlet |
| Portfolio | Protection, guardianship, vigilance, duty |

God of protection and unwavering vigilance – the divine patron of anyone whose job is to stand guard over something and not waver, no matter how long the watch.

### Profile

Disciplined and dutiful rather than glamorous. Clergy often serve as literal guards – of gates, vaults, borders, sacred sites – treating the post itself as an act of worship.

### Faith and Beliefs

Duty fulfilled without deviation, even at personal cost. A Helmite doesn't abandon a post because the situation changed; the post is the point.

### Relationships

Generally respected across the pantheon for reliability rather than allied with any particular faction – a Helmite guard would take the duty seriously regardless of whose treasury or gate they'd been assigned to.

## Oghma, The Binder of What is Known

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | True Neutral |
| Domain(s) | Knowledge |
| Symbol | A blank scroll |
| Portfolio | Knowledge, invention, bards, lore-keeping |

God of knowledge and the spread of it – patron of scholars, sages, bards, and anyone who values a well-kept record over a lost one.

### Profile

Worship centers on libraries, archives, and the act of teaching. Clergy often function as librarians and historians as much as priests.

### Faith and Beliefs

Knowledge should be gathered, preserved, and shared – hoarding it or letting it die with its keeper is treated as a genuine failing.

### Relationships

Loosely allied with any faith that values truth and learning. No strong rivalries – Oghma's faith tends to stay above factional politics.

## Selûne, The Moonmaiden

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Good |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]], [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Light Domain\|Light]] |
| Symbol | A pair of eyes surrounded by seven stars |
| Portfolio | The moon, stars, navigation, good-aligned lycanthropes |

Goddess of the moon and the light it casts in darkness – a guide for travelers and sailors navigating by night, and a protector against the corrupting dark.

### Profile

Night-time rites tied to the moon's phases. Temples often built with open roofs or towers for direct moonlight.

### Faith and Beliefs

Light can exist within darkness rather than only opposing it – a subtly different take on light-and-dark themes than Lathander's dawn-focused faith.

### Relationships

Her sister **Shar** is her opposite and rival – the two are often described as having been locked in conflict since the world's creation.

## Mystra, The Lady of Mysteries

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Good |
| Domain(s) | Knowledge |
| Symbol | A circle of seven stars around a flowing red mist, or simply a seven-pointed star |
| Portfolio | Magic itself, the Weave |

Goddess of magic – not just a patron of magic-users but the literal embodiment of the Weave, the underlying fabric that makes arcane spellcasting possible at all.

### Profile

Worshipped by wizards and sages of virtually any alignment; magic users often pay her respect regardless of their personal faith, given how directly she's tied to their power actually functioning.

### Faith and Beliefs

Magic is a gift to be used responsibly – Mystra has historically taken direct, personal interest in preventing catastrophic misuse of magic on a world-altering scale.

### Relationships

Works closely with Oghma; broadly opposed by anyone seeking to seize or corrupt the Weave itself rather than simply use it.

## Tyr, The Even-Handed, the Maimed God

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Good |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: War Domain\|War]], Knowledge |
| Symbol | A pair of balanced scales resting on a warhammer |
| Portfolio | Justice, law |

God of justice, blind and missing a hand – the maiming is central to his myth, a permanent reminder of the cost of upholding fairness even when it isn't easy or safe.

### Profile

Worshipped by judges, magistrates, and paladins. Clergy often function in a judicial capacity, settling disputes.

### Faith and Beliefs

Justice applied evenly, without favoritism, even when it's costly to the one enforcing it.

### Relationships

Forms a trio with **Torm** and **Ilmater**, sometimes called the Triad – three lawful good gods whose faiths frequently cooperate.

## Bane, The Black Hand, the Lord of Darkness

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Evil |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: War Domain\|War]] |
| Symbol | An upright black right hand, clawed and clenched |
| Portfolio | Tyranny, hatred, fear, control |

God of tyranny – rule through fear and absolute control rather than any pretense of fairness. One of the most commonly invoked patrons of genuinely villainous organizations across the Realms.

### Profile

Worship tends to appear wherever petty tyrants and oppressive regimes need a doctrine to justify themselves – cults of personality, strict hierarchies enforced through terror.

### Faith and Beliefs

Power justifies its own use. The strong rule the weak, and that's simply the correct order of things.

### Relationships

Rival to virtually every good-aligned deity by nature; frequently allied in fiction and lore with other tyrannical or destructive gods.

### Notes

Worth recognizing on sight – Bane's symbol showing up anywhere is a strong signal of genuine, organized villainy rather than simple banditry.

## Ilmater, The Crying God

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Lawful Good |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Life Domain\|Life]] |
| Symbol | Two hands bound at the wrist by a red cord |
| Portfolio | Endurance, suffering, martyrdom, perseverance |

God of endurance through suffering – patron of the oppressed, the enslaved, and anyone enduring hardship not by choice but by circumstance.

### Profile

Clergy often work directly with the suffering: healers, prison ministers, those who tend the poor and the tortured. Some orders practice voluntary suffering in solidarity.

### Faith and Beliefs

Suffering endured without breaking, and relieved wherever possible in others, is itself a holy act.

### Relationships

Part of the Triad with **Tyr** and **Torm** – where they emphasize justice and courage, Ilmater's faith emphasizes compassion for those justice hasn't yet reached.

## Silvanus, Oak Father, the Forest Father

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | True Neutral |
| Domain(s) | Nature |
| Symbol | An oak leaf |
| Portfolio | Wild nature, untamed wilderness |

God of wild nature in its untamed form – less about cultivation like Chauntea, more about the forest that exists whether or not anyone tends it.

### Profile

Druidic circles and rangers are his most common devotees. Worship happens in the wild itself rather than in built temples.

### Faith and Beliefs

Nature's balance matters more than any individual outcome within it – a forest fire and a forest's growth are both part of the same cycle.

### Relationships

Close kinship with **Mielikki** and other nature-aligned deities; a natural counterpoint to anything that would pave over or exploit wilderness without limit.

## Umberlee, The Bitch Queen

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Chaotic Evil |
| Domain(s) | Tempest |
| Symbol | A wave curling left and right |
| Portfolio | The sea, its fury and unpredictability |

Goddess of the sea's destructive, uncaring power – sailors pay her respect not out of devotion but out of genuine fear, tossing coins overboard to buy safe passage.

### Profile

Worship is transactional and fearful rather than loving – offerings made to avoid her wrath, not to earn her favor.

### Faith and Beliefs

The sea owes nothing to anyone, and neither does she. Appeasement is the only sane response.

### Relationships

Feared and grudgingly respected by sailors of every other faith, since ignoring her is considered simply reckless.

## Waukeen, The Merchant's Friend

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | True Neutral |
| Domain(s) | [[z_5.5e Compendium/Classes#Cleric Subclass: Trickery Domain\|Trickery]] |
| Symbol | An upright coin with Waukeen's profile facing left |
| Portfolio | Trade, commerce, wealth through exchange |

Goddess of trade in the broadest sense – fair deals, smuggling, and everything in between, so long as goods and coin are actually changing hands.

### Profile

Popular with merchants, traders, and anyone whose livelihood depends on a good bargain. Worship often overlaps with simple good business sense.

### Faith and Beliefs

Wealth earned through exchange and negotiation is virtuous; wealth hoarded and taken out of circulation is against her nature.

### Relationships

Neutral toward most factions – a Waukeenar cares more about the deal than who's on the other side of it.

## Mielikki, The Forest Queen

### Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Neutral Good |
| Domain(s) | Nature |
| Symbol | A unicorn's head, or a stylized tree |
| Portfolio | Forests, forest creatures, rangers, autumn |

Goddess of forests and the creatures that live within them – gentler and more protective than Silvanus's raw wildness, closer to a guardian of the woodland than an embodiment of it.

### Profile

Rangers, foresters, and druids who see themselves as the forest's caretaker rather than its wild embodiment tend toward her rather than Silvanus.

### Faith and Beliefs

The forest is worth protecting actively, not just left alone – a subtly more interventionist take on nature-worship.

### Relationships

Close kinship with Silvanus; natural opposition to anything that would clear-cut or poison a forest wholesale.

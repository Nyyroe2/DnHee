## Personal Notes

[[The Party]] entered the cave and encountered [[Wolves (Freed Pack)|three wolves]] chained to the wall. [[Mophlin Grimsbourne|Mophlin]] freed them and they found a trash chute behind the wolves leading upwards. [[Hilda Trueshield|Hilda]], Mophlin, and [[Alvar Mistwater|Alvar]] decided to examine the two possible routes up, while [[Willow|Willow]] decided to scout ahead on her own. In her inexperience, she alerted the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] who released their dams, sending the river surging, knocking Hilda down and forcing her out of the cave on the brink of death. Mophlin and [[Alvar Mistwater|Alvar]] went to help Hilda while Willow continued to explore alone. She snuck around but alerted them once more on her way out, causing the goblin's chief to send his personal [[Animals A-Z#Wolf|Wolf]] which bit her.

Meanwhile, Alvar attempted to stabilize Hilda's condition while Mophlin searched for medicinal herbs. Alvar almost killed Hilda faster, but they managed to stabilize her. They decided to wait and rest a bit outside as they were hurt and tired without many other options.

As soon as they felt they had caught their breath, Willow burst from the cave with the [[Animals A-Z#Wolf|Wolf]] chasing after her, though it stood down once it realized its pack was all stood safe and off guard outside. Willow healed herself and Mophlin had the idea to give the wolf a bloody piece of her clothing to try to convince the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] she was killed, which seemingly worked and ended their pursuit of her.

With their wounds mostly healed, they decided to once again enter the cave with the help of the remainder of the [[Wolves (Freed Pack)|wolves]]. Alvar and Hilda tried to climb the trash chute, but Hilda fell, alerting the guards and causing a [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]], the same one who had abused the wolves, to investigate. The [[Wolves (Freed Pack)|wolves]] ripped him to shreds and Mophlin destroyed the whip that had been used on them. Alvar had easily made it up the chute, so Hilda decided to go with Mophlin back up the stream while Willow tried to shimmy after Alvar up the chute. She fell and twisted her ankle, so he lowered a rope to help her up and they entered what seemed to be a sort of storeroom, with a fire in the center and a [[Monsters Library#Bugbears|Bugbear]] who seemed to get their hint with Willow's bloody clothing. Hilda and Mophlin made their way around the other side, curving around the side of the cave before approaching the doorway of a secondary room with more [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] inside. They decided to use Mophlin's axe reflection and Hilda's stonesense to get an idea of their enemies, while Willow snuck around to the other side of the room her and Alvar were hiding inside to do the same. The group readied themselves for battle.

## Session Summary

As we continued our journey on the wagon to [[Phandalin Overview|Phandalin]], we were joined by a small gnome barbarian who could only speak gibberish that took Nox's place on our journey. As we continued, Alvar lost the horses that were attached to the wagon, and while the little gnome weasel tried to convince some new horses to join them, they warned him of [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] hiding in the trees. Four [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] jumped out and attacked them and they began [[Session 1 - Goblin Skirmish|combat]]. After the fight, they tried to get new horses to help with the journey, but Hilda accidentally hurt them while trying to patch them up, so Willow summoned some deer that little gnome weasel convinced to pull the wagon in exchange for carrots, and they continued on their way.

The party arrived at Phandalin, and visited the shop Alvar was intended to deliver things to, [[Shop - Barthen's Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]]. Alvar told them that Hilda and little gnome weasel helped, but excluded Willow as he felt she had been unhelpful, so the three of them got paid 12 gp each. Little gnome weasel bought an empty book to write in so he could communicate, and he and Hilda made plans for her to help him learn to communicate better. Little gnome weasel gave Willow 5 gp to try to make up for Alvar excluding her, and they gave carrots to the deer that pulled the cart.

The group moved on, and while they were walking along the little gnome weasel used his journal to tell Hilda his name was Mophlin. They made their way into a weapons shop, [[Shop - Lionshield Coster|Lionshield Coster]], where Hilda bought a crowbar, Mophlin bought some blow darts and scale mail armor, and Alvar exchanged his shortsword for a rapier. The shop keep, [[Linene Graywind]] complained about [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] stopping shipments, and requested that Hilda take care of them in exchange for 50 gp. Hilda invited the group to come along, and they decided to go to a local inn, [[Inn - Stonehill Inn|Stonehill Inn]], by Linene's suggestion.

After Alvar got everyone rooms sorted and some ales, Mophlin proposed an arm wrestle with Alvar, who simply shook his hand and did not understand the motion. To teach him what an arm wrestle was, Mophlin proposed an arm wrestle with a random large stranger nearby. Mophlin, the gnome, absolutely demolishes his ass and gets ale for the man as an apology for ruining his self esteem. Alvar seems to understand the concept, and immediately gets his ass kicked by a kid in his first arm wrestle. Mophlin finds himself listening to the woes of the drunken stranger, who also tells Mophlin that the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] are working with the marauders somewhere near Cragmore. The kid, [[Pip]], also tells Alvar about a secret tunnel his friend [[Carp]], son of the farmer, found that leads into the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]]'s camp.

Hilda and Willow have a heart to heart about Willow's fears for the forest, as she reveals she recently left her home for the first time after many of the animals living there inexplicably disappeared. Hilda tries to console her before they all go upstairs and have a long rest. Alvar and Mophlin relay with the group about what they heard the night before, and they decide to go to [[Residence - Alderleaf Farm|Alderleaf Farm]]. The party meets Carp and Alvar asks him about the tunnel, and he says he saw it at a nearby manor, but he ran when someone told him to scram. He came upon a [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]] with a letter and took it. He hid it but it said something about a hideout on the road into the town.

The party decides to go and investigate the hideout, coming across what seems to be the opening of a cave with a river flowing out of it. Hilda crosses to the entrance to attempt to use her stonesense to feel out the cave, but encounters three [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]]. She also picks up on the presence of 22 additional enemies within range inside the cave, and shouts the count to her team. They engage in [[Session 1 - Goblin Hideout Entrance|combat]] at the entrance and then prepare to continue to enter the dark cave.

## Personal Notes

The party went to [[Locations/Phandalin/Shop - Lionshield Coster|Lionshield Coster]] and returned [[Linene Graywind|Linene]]'s goods. She was shocked they'd managed to get them back, and paid them the 50 gp she'd promised. She told them they had probably noticed the [[Groups/Redbrands|Redbrands]], mercenaries who have been helping the goblins and are staying at the Sleeping Giant taphouse, though she let them know the Redbrands would definitely be missed. They had also come into her shop asking about old mines, specifically the old Phandelver mines. The party asked her about [[Session Notes/Rumors/The Spider|The Spider]], but she didn't know much. Willow wandered into her kitchen and took some bread. Mophlin used a page from his journal to write out "can you help me speak to my dogs please" so he can ask for help without casting speak with animals every time. Linene recommended he visit the Shrine of Luck to Tymora.

They went on to [[Locations/Phandalin/Shop - Barthen's Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]], since Alvar wanted to double check exactly what had been in the wagon and see if the shop knew anything more. The owner, [[Elmina]], told him Gundren was her friend, so the party started questioning her, and she grew suspicious right away and shut down. Alvar followed her into her shop and pleaded with her to tell them what she knew, because Gundren is the last tie he has to his family. She quizzed him on personal details and asked where Gundren's birthmark is. She then said that wherever the Redbrands are, they would probably find Gundren, and the party decided to leave.

At the [[Locations/Phandalin/Shrine - Tymora|Shrine of Tymora]] they met [[Sister Garaele]], a young half-elf woman in priestess robes with weathered eyes. Hilda and Willow went up to ask about Willow's dagger, though Willow was very shy. Garaele took the dagger carefully and examined it, and offered to investigate it if the party did a favor for her. She said she works for the [[Groups/Harpers|Harpers]], who have a mission that needs finishing: persuade a banshee named [[Bestiary/Agatha|Agatha]] to say where a spellbook is. The book used to belong to a powerful mage. Garaele suggested they butter Agatha up and compliment her, and gave them a valuable [[Items/Silver Comb|silver comb]] to trade for an answer, one question per gift. She said the banshee attacked her because she reminded Agatha of her old love.

Hilda then helped Mophlin ask Garaele about talking to his wolves. Garaele said he knows more than he realizes, and that she would help him communicate with them once the banshee business was done.

The party decided to confront the mercenaries at the taphouse. On the way, Willow went into a side house, found a child's teddy bear, and took it. Hilda realized she'd taken a child's toy, scolded her until she put it back, and handed her the toy from [[Klarg's Loot - Session 3|Klarg's loot]] instead. At the taphouse door the Redbrands started heckling Willow, and she threw the toy at one of them, which started a fight.

Mophlin challenged one of them to an arm wrestle and had his arm broken. Willow hit one of the closer Redbrands with poison spray, and he turned and swung at Alvar. Alvar answered with burning hands, killing the man who attacked him and injuring another, then used second wind. Hilda healed Mophlin's arm with healing word and swung her morningstar, landing squarely in the center of a Redbrand's chest. Mophlin swung his greataxe at the man who had beaten him at arm wrestling, taking his hand off at the wrist and pinning him to the table, and his wolves dragged the man away and ate him alive. Willow missed the same man Hilda had hit, and Alvar missed too and landed on his own sword. The two remaining mercenaries swung at Willow and missed.

The bartender shouted for the fight to be taken outside and moved like he was finally standing up to the mercenaries. Hilda missed her next swing, and the party decided to just knock out the last one.

The owner thanked them and told them the Redbrands' hideout is the old [[Locations/Phandalin/Tressinder Manor|Tressinder Manor]] on the hill, and that it's where they take prisoners. The party rested in the Sleeping Giant's taproom, which the mercenaries had taken over until they were cleared out, and Hilda split the 50 gp evenly, 12 gp 5 sp each. Alvar went to help repair the doors they'd broken. They tried to pay him 5 gp, but he gave it back, so the large orc bartender offered to help them at Tressinder Manor instead.

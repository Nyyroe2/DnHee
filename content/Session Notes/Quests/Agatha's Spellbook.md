[[NPCs/Sister Garaele]], who works for the [[Groups/Harpers|Harpers]], wants the party to persuade a [[Compendium/Monsters Library#Banshee|banshee]] named [[Bestiary/Agatha|Agatha]] to say where a spellbook is. The book used to belong to a powerful mage.

## Beginning

Hilda and Willow asked Garaele about Willow's dagger at the [[Locations/Phandalin/Shrine - Tymora|Shrine of Tymora]]. She offered to investigate it if they did this favor for her. She suggested they butter Agatha up and compliment her to get her talking, and gave them a valuable [[Items/Silver Comb|silver comb]] to trade for an answer. Per Garaele, each gift buys one question.

## Relationships

**[[NPCs/Sister Garaele]]**: Gave the party the job.
**[[Bestiary/Agatha|Agatha]]**: The banshee to question. She attacked Garaele because Garaele reminded her of her old love.

### Rewards

- Garaele will help Mophlin communicate with his wolves.

## Summary

[[Monsters Library#Goblins|Goblins]] have set up camp nearby and are attacking travellers and shipments coming and going from the town. The party was asked to go into their camp and wipe them out, while retrieving the stock for the [[Shop - Lionshield Coster|Lionshield Coster]].

### Rewards

- 50 gp, paid in Session 5 and split evenly (12 gp 5 sp each)

## Background

The [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] have previously attacked us as well

## Additional Details

Session 1:

- The party met a young boy who gave them directions to the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] hideout
- They were warned that the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] seem to be working with the marauders and working out of Cragmore
- They found the entrance to the caves where the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] live and killed some of them that were stationed outside

Session 2:

- The party entered the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]]'s [[The Goblin Hideout|Goblin Cave]], killing any they met

Session 3:

- The party killed the bugbear in charge of the goblins, [[Bestiary/Klarg|Klarg]], and recovered the stock for [[Linene Graywind|Linene]].
- They discovered [[Sildar Hallwinter]] imprisoned inside, and upon healing him learned that he was also searching for [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]
- Sildar told us that [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] had been taken to the manor acting as their main boss. He begs us to help him look into the disappearance of his friend.

Session 5:

- The party returned Linene's goods and were paid the 50 gp reward.

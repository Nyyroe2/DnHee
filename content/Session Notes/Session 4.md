## Personal Notes

[[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]], who the party saved, told them he's from the [[Groups/Lords Alliance|Lords Alliance]]. He seemed worried about something, and told them about a strange goblin with an elongated head who said he "wasn't who they wanted." It seems connected to [[Wave Echo Cave|Wave Echo Cave]], a place he said [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] had found, but whatever Gundren learned was lost when he went missing. Sildar asked if they knew about the "Phandelver's Pact," an old agreement about Wave Echo Cave that holds something called the Forge of Spells, usable by people of all races. He thinks that's why Gundren was taken. When Alvar pressed for more, Sildar said someone calling themself "[[Session Notes/Rumors/The Spider|The Spider]]" had asked to see Gundren.

The party agreed to help Sildar search for Gundren and Iano, and realized the manor he'd described sounded like the same one [[Carp]] had been chased off from back in [[Session Notes/Session 1|Session 1]]. Before chasing that lead into what they expected would be a harder fight, they decided the smarter move was to swing by [[Locations/Phandalin/Shop - Lionshield Coster|Lionshield Coster]] first, both to return [[Linene Graywind|Linene]]'s recovered stock from the goblin hideout and to see about better gear while they had the chance.

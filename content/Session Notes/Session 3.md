# Session 3

## Summary

The party cleared the remaining rooms of the goblin hideout with help from Mophlin's wolf pack, losing one wolf but killing the bugbear chief and the last of the goblins. Searching the lair turned up a new morningstar for Hilda and a letter exposing a kidnapping plot against one of Alvar's friends; they found and freed a captive already held in the cave and agreed to escort him back to Phandalin for 50 gp, leveling up in the process.

## Personal Notes

Mophlin and Hilda prepare to enter the fight with a quick prayer, granting them both [[Spells#Bless|bless]], while Alvar and Willow both attempt to kill a [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]], though both miss. Their stealth mission is saved, however, by a tiny angry [[Compendium/Races/Gnome|gnome]] screaming and running into the next room over, flinging an axe into the throat of one of the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]]. This enrages one of the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]], who drives his scimitar through the neck of one of Mophlin's [[Wolves (Freed Pack)|wolves]], killing it instantly. Alvar tries to take the opportunity to attack the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]], but misses once again, alerting them to him and Willow's position. Willow flings a vat of oil at them, casting flaming oil across them and burning a wolf, which turns and bites into her neck, downing her. Hilda enters the room behind Mophlin, casting [[Spells#Sacred Flame|sacred flame]] to burn off the arm of one of the [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]]. Mophlin, enraged, finished off the other two [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblins]] in the room before laying his cloak over the body of his deceased furry friend. They follow the sounds of fighting into the next room, where Hilda casts [[Spells#Healing Word|Healing Word]] to bring back Willow, who panics and brandishes her glowing dagger against the wolf still covered in her blood. Mophlin rushes in and swings for the [[Monsters Library#Bugbears|bugbear]] boss, while Alvar finally lands a hit on dumb dumb doblin, driving his rapier directly up his stomach and out his mouth. Hilda uses [[Spells#Sacred Flame|sacred flame]] to kill the [[Monsters Library#Bugbears|bugbear]] in defence of Mophlin, burning his head from the inside out. Willow scrambles to her feet and throws herself at the last [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]], getting attacked by the wolf as she slices his head off. Mophlin turns to try to defuse things with the [[Monsters Library#Bugbears|Bugbear]] chief's wolf, and tries to speak with him. He manages to intimidate him into disengaging, and the party levels up.

The party searched the room, and Hilda got a new morningstar, among other items. They found a letter about the [[Monsters Library#Bugbears|bugbear]] planning to kidnap Alvar's friend, so Hilda used stonesense to find out another person was kidnapped and they went to him. Hilda healed him and they assisted him out of the cave, and he told them his story.

The party agreed to escort him back to Phandalin for 50 gp

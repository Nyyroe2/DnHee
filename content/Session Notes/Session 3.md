## Personal Notes

Mophlin and Hilda prepared to enter the fight with a quick prayer, which granted them both a [[Spells#Bless|blessing]].  On the other side of the caves, Alvar and Willow both attempted to sneak up on and kill a [[Monsters Library#Goblins|goblin]], though both their attacks missed. Their stealth mission was saved, however, by the massive distraction Mophlin caused one room over. After entering a rage, he sprinted into the room and flung an axe into the throat of one of the goblins. This angered one of the other goblins, who responded by turning on Mophlin's nearby wolves. He drove his scimitar through the neck of one of Mophlin's [[Wolves (Freed Pack)|wolves]], which killed it instantly. Alvar tried to use the distraction as an opportunity to attack the goblins, but missed once again, which finally alerted the other goblins to him and Willow's position. Willow responded by flinging a vat of flaming oil at them, which hit and burned the chief's wolf. In a panic, it responded by biting into her neck, downing her.

Mophlin rushed further into the cavern with Hilda on his heels, who cast [[Spells#Sacred Flame|sacred flame]] to burn off the arm of one of the goblins. Saddened and enraged at the death of his wolf friend, Mophlin finished off the other two goblins in the room before laying his cloak over the body of his deceased furry friend. They followed the sounds of fighting into the next room, where Hilda saw Willow's body limp in the maw of the wolf and cast [[Spells#Healing Word|Healing Word]] to bring her back. She immediately panicked and brandished her glowing dagger against the wolf still covered in her blood. Mophlin rushed in and swung for the [[Bestiary/Klarg|bugbear]] boss, which responded with an attack of its own, while Alvar finally landed a hit on dumb dumb doblin, driving his rapier directly up his stomach and out through his mouth. Hilda used [[Spells#Sacred Flame|sacred flame]] to kill the bugbear in defence of Mophlin, burning his brain from the inside out. Willow scrambled to her feet in a panic and threw herself at the last goblin, which incited the chief's now ownerless wolf to attempt to lung at her one last time. It missed, and her dagger hits true as she managed to slice the last goblin's head off. With the dead chief's wolf the last enemy standing, Mophlin turned to try to defuse things with him, casting speak with animals. His plea came off more scary than he hoped, but he managed to intimidate him into disengaging, and the party levels up.

The party searched the room, and Hilda got a new Morningstar, among other [[Klarg's Loot - Session 3|items]]. They also found [[Items/Klarg's Letter|a letter]] about [[Bestiary/Klarg|Klarg]] planning to kidnap Alvar's friend, [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]], so Hilda used stonesense again to check if anyone else was nearby. They discovered another person back towards the cave entrance, and when they go to investigate, they find [[Sildar Hallwinter]], an agent of the [[Groups/Lords Alliance|Lords Alliance]]. He explains that he was searching for his friend, Gundren, but the goblins had moved him to their main headquarters before he had arrived. He promised to go into detail after we get back and offers to pay us 50 gp to escort him.

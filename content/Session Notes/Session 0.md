## Session Summary

Everyone gathered at a tavern in Neverwinter for the night in preparation for their trip to Phandalin. Hilda was sat alone, enjoying her meal, when Willow approached her and struck up a conversation. When Hilda noticed the girls' lack of food, she bought her a dinner of cheeses and bread, after Willow opted out of  having meat due to being a vegetarian. Hilda lectured the girl on making sure to eat proper meals and avoid sinning, before retiring for the night.

The following morning, Hilda boarded the wagon following her morning prayers alongside Willow, while Nox and Alvar took the reins. Though Alvar was the intended driver for the journey, he needed the assistance of more experienced hands and a bit of prayer before the party could set off on their journey.

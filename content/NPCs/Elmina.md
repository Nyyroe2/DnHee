### "The Wary Shopkeeper"

> _Gundren's friend, and not about to trust a stranger asking about him._

**Owner of Barthen's Provisions** · From: [[Phandalin Overview|Phandalin]] · Status: Alive · Trust: Neutral

## Personality & Demeanor

Seemed kind when the party delivered the wagon. When they started asking about Gundren she grew suspicious and shut down, and she only talked once Alvar pleaded with her and answered her personal questions.

## First Encounter

The party delivered the wagon of provisions to her shop, [[Locations/Phandalin/Shop - Barthen's Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]], when they arrived in Phandalin ([[Session Notes/Session 1|Session 1]]), and she paid them for the delivery. They learned her name in [[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]]. "Barthen's Provisions" is just the name of the store, and whether Barthen is her surname is unknown.

## Known For

- Telling the party that [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] is her friend, then clamming up when they pressed her (Session 5).
- Saying that wherever the [[Groups/Redbrands|Redbrands]] are, the party is likely to find Gundren (Session 5).

## Relationships

**[[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]**: Her friend.
**[[Player Characters/Alvar/Alvar Mistwater|Alvar]]**: Quizzed him on personal details, including where Gundren's birthmark is, before she would say anything about Gundren.

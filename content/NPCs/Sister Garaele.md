# Sister Garaele

### "The Priestess of Luck"

> _A young half-elf priestess of Tymora who works for the Harpers, and wants a favor._

**[[Compendium/Races/Elf|Half-Elf]]** · **Priestess of Tymora** · From: [[Locations/Phandalin/Shrine - Tymora|Shrine - Tymora]] · Status: Alive · Trust: Friendly · Faith: [[Deities#Tymora, Lady Luck|Tymora]]

## Appearance

A young half-elf woman in priestess robes, with weathered eyes.

## Personality & Demeanor

Careful with the things she's handed, and practical about what she wants in return. She offered to look into Willow's dagger only after the party agreed to do a favor for her.

## First Encounter

The party found her at the [[Locations/Phandalin/Shrine - Tymora|Shrine of Tymora]] in [[Session Notes/Session 5|Session 5]], after [[Linene Graywind|Linene]] recommended Mophlin visit it. Hilda and Willow approached her about Willow's dagger, and Hilda later helped Mophlin ask about talking to his wolves.

## Known For

- Examining Willow's glowing dagger and offering to investigate it if the party does a favor for her (Session 5).
- Telling the party she works for the [[Groups/Harpers|Harpers]], who have a mission to finish: get the [[Compendium/Monsters Library#Banshee|banshee]] [[Bestiary/Agatha|Agatha]] to say where a mage's spellbook is.
- Giving the party a valuable [[Items/Silver Comb|silver comb]] to trade for an answer from Agatha, one question per gift.
- Saying Mophlin knows more than he realizes about talking to his wolves, and promising to help him communicate with them once the banshee is dealt with.

## Relationships

**[[Bestiary/Agatha|Agatha]]**: The banshee attacked her because she reminded Agatha of her old love.
**[[Groups/Harpers|The Harpers]]**: She works for them.
**[[Player Characters/Willow/Willow|Willow]]**: Looked at her dagger.
**[[Player Characters/Mophlin/Mophlin Grimsbourne|Mophlin]]**: Will help him talk to his wolves once the banshee is dealt with.
